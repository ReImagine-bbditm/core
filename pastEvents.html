Alumni Talk

Date
30 November 2024
Venue
Online, hosted by BBDITM

An online session with Mr. Shivam Kesharwani, Senior Associate at Cognizant Worldwide Limited, UK. It connected current students with an alumnus for a real-world view of careers and the IT industry.

Highlights

  • Industry insights: current trends, tools, and practices, especially in a global work environment.
  • Career guidance: planning a career, building skills, and moving from college to work.
  • Global exposure: what working in the UK is like, based on his own experience.
  • Networking: students were encouraged to reach out to alumni for mentorship and advice.
  • Inspiration: a successful graduate's journey as a model for ambitious goals.

Industrial Visit to STPI

Date
25 November 2024
Venue
STPI

Students visited the Plug and Play (PNP) hall, which hosts seminars, workshops, and conferences, then the Network Operating Centre that monitors and manages IT infrastructure, and finished with a presentation on STPI in the conference room.

Highlights

  • Presentation on STPI's mission, vision, infrastructure, and facilities.
  • Guided tour of the incubation centre and data centre.
  • Question and answer session with STPI officials.
  • Mr. Satyam explained STPI's objectives and services, including office space, funding support of up to ₹25 lakh, and the Plug and Play model.
  • Ms. Disha Srivastava took students through STPI's core infrastructure.
  • Mr. Jairam Yadav presented STPI's operating model and opportunities for startups.
