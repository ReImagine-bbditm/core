About us

Who we are

ReImagine is a student-led club at BBDITM focused on technology, innovation, and skill building. We bring together students who want to learn by making things.

Our mission

We give students a collaborative space to learn, experiment, and apply their skills to real-world problems.

Who can join

ReImagine is open to students of the Departments of Information Technology and Data Science from second year onwards. Beginners and experienced members are both welcome.

  • Student-led

    Members plan and run every event.

  • Learn by doing

    Workshops and projects, not just talks.

  • Industry exposure

    Visits and talks from working professionals.

  • Alumni network

    Graduates share advice with current students.
